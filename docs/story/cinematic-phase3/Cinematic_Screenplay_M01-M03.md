# WORLD FRACTURE — CINEMATIC SCREENPLAY / PHASE 2

Production draft for Missions 01–03. Preserve all existing 25 missions and legacy encounters.

## Directing principles
- Player is an ordinary civilian at the start. No mandatory calibration or training mission.
- Use player-selected avatar appearance; storyboard actors are placeholders.
- Cinematics transition into gameplay at rescue and street-combat beats.
- NOVA is a voice/interface, not a fixed human character model.
- Timing estimates are editorial placeholders, not recorded performance measurements.
