# Ilyra Sen — The Voice of the Concord
Faction: Elyndra Concord | Role: Supreme leader | Combat identity: Celestial Sovereign

## Visual identity
Realistic human female; long silver-white hair; dignified, compassionate but formidable expression. White, silver and restrained gold celestial armor with layered articulated plates, blue energy channels and a luminous chest core. Signature weapon: energy-forged spear/lance. Palette: pearl white, silver, gold, blue.

## 3D asset requirements
- Full-body high-detail hero mesh; separate head, hair cards/groom, armor plates, cloth/cape and weapon.
- PBR material set: base color, normal, roughness, metallic, ambient occlusion and emissive.
- Humanoid body skeleton; facial blendshapes or equivalent facial rig; cloth/hair secondary motion.
- LOD0 hero, LOD1 gameplay, LOD2 distance; collision; clean UVs; sensible modular armor sockets.
- Animation set: idle, locomotion, turns, combat locomotion, spear combo, thrust, block, Aegis Dominion cast, hit reactions, stagger, defeat, cinematic gestures.
- Export targets: FBX for engine pipeline and GLB for review. No actual mesh/rig has been generated yet.

## Gameplay signature
Defensive area control, gravity manipulation and precise radiant spear attacks. VFX remain separate from the base mesh.
