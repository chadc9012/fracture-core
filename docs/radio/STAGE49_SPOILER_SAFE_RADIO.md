# Stage 49 — Spoiler-Safe In-World Radio

## Broadcast policy
Fracture FM now airs only **Episodes 06–08**, new original caller shows focused on ordinary civic issues, competing neighborhood factions and uncertain Fracture-related weather. They use fictional characters and fictional reports, NOT verified live simulation events. The presenters distinguish government statements, callers' allegations and unknowns. **No main quest outcomes, hidden encounters, boss identities, bond rewards, locations of secrets, or explanations of the Fracture are disclosed.**

Older Episodes 01–05 remain in the archive but are **not in the live playlist** pending a full editorial review. The WyrmShield dragon-themed ad is similarly **excluded from broadcast** pending spoiler review. Do not re-add them without checking scripts and audio.

## New media
- Episodes 06 Council Hotline, 07 Faction Open Line, 08 Fracture Weather Watch; original multi-voice synthetic demos.
- Five original fictional ads: Fracture Safety Office, Market Mile, Circuit Cafe, Patchwork Repairs, Nexus Community Board.
- Three new 96-second original instrumental tracks: Night Shift (old school), Night Drive (instrumental), After Hours (house).
- Existing user-supplied Kinggg Dream tracks and their station assignments preserved, including Keep Moving on Neon Frequency.

## Radio mechanics
Each built-in station is a linear broadcast. Songs and episodes play fully without skip or shuffle. A commercial plays after every two programs. The game now rotates 9 spoiler-reviewed ad files. The now-playing display labels the new programs and tracks.

## Editorial and technical limitations
No actual game-state event subscription or live faction/government feed exists yet. Episodes describe *fictional ambient public chatter*, not dynamically verified state. Voice acting is synthesized, songs are instrumentals, and there are no newly licensed third-party songs. The full Vite build and browser playback are still unverified. Old audio files remain in the ZIP for archival use but are unreachable via the current broadcast playlist.
