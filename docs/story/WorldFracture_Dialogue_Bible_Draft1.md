# WORLD FRACTURE — STORY DIALOGUE BIBLE (DRAFT 1)

**Scope:** 25 proposed main-story missions, branch variations, ambient barks, combat, vehicles and companions. This is an original *first-pass campaign dialogue draft*, not proof of existing game canon or an exhaustive script for every legacy boss, dungeon, hidden encounter, activity or quest. All existing 18 hidden encounter unlocks, rewards, Vyrak trust milestones and previous content remain protected.

**Player character:** Gender-neutral lines; voice and visual identity determined by character creator. **NOVA:** precise, restrained, compassionate AI; **Vyrak:** proud, ceremonial, speaks in capitals; other voices distinct.

**Voice production:** No recorded audio or lip sync is included. IDs in `dialogue.json` are stable keys for localization, recording and Godot integration. Branch dialogue is conditional, not a replacement for existing quest logic.


## Mission 01 — Apartment 12-04
*Region: Nexus City. Story beat: The civilian morning and first Fracture.*
**NEWS:** Good morning, Nexus City. Overnight, residents reported unusual lights over the harbor.

**PLAYER:** Another ordinary morning. That would be nice.

**NEWS:** Officials say there is no cause for alarm—

**PLAYER:** The windows are shaking.

**NOVA:** Neural resonance confirmed. Can you hear me?

**PLAYER:** Who is this?

**NOVA:** NOVA. Your building is destabilizing. Leave the apartment now.

**PLAYER:** What happened to the sky?

**NOVA:** A Fracture. Reality is intersecting with something it was never meant to touch.

**PLAYER:** There are people trapped in the hall.

**NOVA:** Then we help them. Move. I will guide you.

**CIVILIAN:** Please! The stairwell is blocked!

**PLAYER:** Get behind me. I think I can move this.

**NOVA:** That energy came from you. We will understand it later.

**PLAYER:** Later sounds good. Where is the exit?

## Mission 05 — The Choice to Stay

## Production notes

- For cinematic implementation, assign each line a scene camera, animation, audio clip, subtitle duration and trigger; none of these are authored in the current game.
- Each branching choice requires a corresponding existing quest state flag, consequence and save/load test before shipping.
- Avoid mandatory Verdant Forest calibration after Mission 1.
- Vyrak riding unlocks only after full trust, never at initial bond.
- Thalassia remains offshore from Verdant Forest; Neon City is distinct from Nexus City.
- Legacy bosses, dungeons, 18 hidden encounters and existing rewards are not replaced by this narrative outline.
- Full-game coverage still requires an editable project and quest database to identify every actual mission, side quest, boss, NPC and dialogue trigger.
