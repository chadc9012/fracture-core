# Auren Thale — The First Cartographer
Faction: Elyndra Concord | Role: Explorer and dimensional researcher | Combat identity: Rift Pathfinder

## Visual identity
Athletic male explorer with practical layered silver, bronze and blue field armor, travel-worn details, mapping equipment and a dimensional staff/tool. Curious, alert expression. Palette: silver, bronze, muted blue.

## 3D asset requirements
- Full-body athletic hero mesh; modular field armor, harness, pouches, gloves, boots and staff.
- Face and hair groom; readable silhouette at gameplay distance; functional gear attachment points.
- PBR materials with restrained wear, fabric and metal differentiation; holographic map effects separate from geometry.
- Humanoid skeleton and facial rig; staff socket; secondary motion for straps and cloth.
- Animation set: explorer idle, walk/run, scan/map interaction, staff combat, rift step, spatial anchor cast, climb/land, hit reactions and cinematic gestures.
- LODs, collision, UVs; FBX and GLB targets. Current reference is a portrait/concept image, not a model.
