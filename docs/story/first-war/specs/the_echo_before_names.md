# The Echo Before Names — The First Consciousness
Faction: Elyndra / Origin | Role: Primordial guide | Combat identity: Reality Architect

## Visual identity
Ancient, dignified male-presenting consciousness with long silver-white locks and beard, luminous golden eyes, subtle glowing runes and layered white/silver/gold ceremonial armor or robes. Ethereal golden-violet energy rings. Must feel ancient and composed, not a conventional brute.

## 3D asset requirements
- Full-body humanoid base mesh with layered robe/armor pieces and separate hair/beard groom.
- Optional ethereal/translucent shader variants; emissive rune masks and energy rings as separate VFX assets.
- Facial rig for restrained, wise expressions; cloth and hair secondary motion.
- Humanoid skeleton with optional floating/hover locomotion variant; hand sockets for energy manifestations.
- Animation set: calm idle, slow walk/hover, gesture, memory projection, reality reflection, temporal fragment, energy channel, dissolve/reform, cinematic speaking.
- LODs, collision, UVs; FBX and GLB targets. No actual mesh/rig has been generated yet.
