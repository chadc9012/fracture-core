# The Veiled Sovereign — Founder of the Unbound
Faction: The Unbound | Role: Founder and commander | Combat identity: Paradox Strategist

## Visual identity
Commanding female fighter with hood, silver-white braids, green eyes, subtle facial markings, dark bronze/silver articulated armor and a weathered cloak with green emissive accents. Signature weapon: green dimensional blade. Preserve anonymity and a concealed identity in story-facing variants.

## 3D asset requirements
- Full-body hero mesh with separate hood, face covering option, hair, cloak, armor and weapon.
- Cloth simulation or authored secondary motion for cloak; braid physics; facial rig that supports guarded expressions.
- PBR dark metal, worn leather/fabric and green emissive materials.
- Humanoid skeleton, facial rig, weapon sockets and modular hood/face variants.
- Animation set: stealth idle, walk/run, evasive movement, blade combo, ghost protocol, trap placement, adaptive counter, dimensional collapse cast, cloak-aware turns.
- LODs, collision, UVs; FBX and GLB targets. No actual mesh/rig has been generated yet.
