# Vael Turog — The Severant
Faction: Null Choir | Role: Supreme military commander | Combat identity: Reality Executioner

## Visual identity
Massive, imposing male warlord; battle-scarred face; long dark hair; black obsidian heavy armor with violet emissive channels and severe angular silhouette. Signature weapon: oversized dimensional cleaver/blade. Palette: obsidian black, gunmetal, violet.

## 3D asset requirements
- Hero body mesh with broad proportions and layered modular heavy armor.
- Separate helmet/face options, shoulder plates, gauntlets, greaves, weapon and armor damage variants.
- PBR textures including emissive violet channels; robust deformation around shoulders, elbows, hips and knees.
- Humanoid skeleton, facial rig, weapon sockets and optional armor cloth/strap simulation.
- Animation set: heavy idle, deliberate walk/run, turns, heavy combo, overhead cleave, lunge, suppression field cast, stagger, phase transition, defeat.
- LODs, collision, clean UVs; FBX and GLB targets. No actual mesh/rig has been generated yet.

## Gameplay signature
Heavy melee pressure, ability suppression zones and tactical adaptation. Suppression VFX are separate from the armor mesh.
