# Game rules (src/game and the 3D game components)

- Keep camera preference client-local: FPS is default, F persists FPS/TPP, and melee or special actions temporarily override to TPP; camera choice is presentation, not shared combat state.
- Player progression syncs to the player_saves table via optimistic revision checks; conflicts merge additively (unions/maxes), newer copy wins choices. Why: progress is only gained, so merging never loses unlocks.
- Keep regional enemy and boss identities in a shared encounter catalog, with material drops granted into progression on confirmed kills. Why: map intelligence, combat rewards, and inventory must agree.
- Authored missions are pure state machines in src/game/missions/* advanced by world events emitted from Scene (ANCHOR/ARRIVED/CLEAR) and UI (HACK/ACK). Why: deterministic, testable, no menu-driven quest flow.
- Mission order: awakening → broken-signal → drowned-relay (swamps, fd-05) → blackout-protocol → stitched-neon-core → solar-array (solara, fd-08) → core-node (nexus, fd-09: NOVA reveal + faction choice in story.choices.faction, which picks the ending via src/game/endings.ts) → frozen-beacon (fd-11) → failure-core (fd-13) → convoy-breaker (fd-14) → descent-protocol; the last three share one machine in missions/zone-missions.ts (data per mission, start gated by zoneMissionReady on the previous chapter quest) → system-core. A new zone mission joins MISSION_IDS/RESUMABLE_STATES/RESPAWNS_ON_RESUME/COMPLETION/PREREQUISITE in missions/persistence.ts and gets a missionSuite test; quest objectives are matched by index, so add new objectives after existing ones. Why: saves resume safely and part-way quest progress is never misread.
- Input bindings live in src/game/bindings.ts and persist client-side (localStorage "world-fracture-bindings"); Scene reads them each frame for keyboard + standard-mapping gamepads. Why: controls are per-device presentation, not progression.
- Cloud restore points are captured by a database trigger on player_saves updates (max 1 per 10 min, keep 30); restoring replaces rather than merges. Why: the backend guarantees history even if a client misbehaves.
- Combat audio remains procedural, while spoken story audio uses one prioritized, cancellable voice director and server-streamed synthesis with captions as fallback. Why: story lines never overlap, credentials stay private, and gameplay remains usable when speech is muted or unavailable.
- Map/HUD markers share src/game/waypoints.ts so compass, map and beacons agree; creation stays a spatial Identity Forge while preserving saved identity contracts.
- Destructible interiors use the structural graph in src/game/destruction.ts; every change is logged as a DestructionEvent. Why: future multiplayer replicates events, not physics.
- Dense authored districts share street furniture and animated transit from DistrictStreet.tsx; district files own their local architecture. Why: Thalassia and Neon City stay visually related without coupling their layouts.
- World model URLs must resolve all dependent textures; repaired NPC GLBs bundle without missing external maps, and unreachable rock models are excluded from preloading. Why: a failed model can suspend the entire 3D scene and leave only the HUD visible.
- The world Canvas is wrapped in GraphicsGuard (src/game/webgl-support.ts probe): no WebGL, context loss, or no frames shows GraphicsError; Safari gets capped DPR, hard shadows, no post-processing. Why: Safari failures otherwise leave a black canvas.
- Weather is a deterministic per-region front cycle in src/game/weather-cycle.ts sampled from the day clock; Scene applies it to light/fog/particles. Why: every client agrees on weather without syncing.
- Enemy awareness (patrol/suspicious/alert/search, cover) is a pure state machine in src/game/enemy-perception.ts driven by sim.ts. Why: detection rules stay testable apart from movement.
- Major player-facing transitions use explicit full-screen phases, while in-world utility surfaces remain mutually exclusive overlays. Why: onboarding, deployment, and navigation stay legible without stacked interfaces.
- Speech-driven faces/gestures read a shared live envelope from src/game/voice-animation.ts; the voice director owns one audio graph + LRU line cache. Why: no per-line contexts, no frame stalls.
- Retention rules (chronicle, next activity, recap, repeat reward taper) live in src/game/retention.ts. Why: testable, no content is ever removed.
- Region image-based lighting uses verified Poly Haven HDRIs via RegionLighting with a Lightformer fallback. Why: a dead asset must never suspend the scene.
- Mission payouts go through rewardMission in src/game/progression.ts (first-clear bonus + daily repeat taper). Why: one place enforces anti-grind rules.
- Respawn/checkpoint rules live in src/game/respawn.ts; Scene records checkpoints only on calm safe ground. Why: never respawn into active combat.
- Save slots (src/game/save-slots.ts) park inactive games in player_save_slots; the active slot is the single player_saves row. Why: reuses the existing sync and restore-point pipeline.
- Per-class weapon loadouts live in progression.arsenal and are passed to Scene as weaponOrder. Why: loadouts are progression, weapon feel stays in weapons.ts.
- Region ground textures and extra models come from src/game/region-materials.ts and RegionModels.tsx; each asset is verified and isolated so failures fall back silently.
- Seasons and environmental hazards are pure functions in src/game/environment.ts (seasons from the day clock, thermal index, exposure meters, telegraphed lightning), stepped by Scene and applied via sim.ts. Why: every client agrees without syncing, rules stay testable apart from rendering.
- Stratagems (Helldivers-style call-ins) are a pure code-entry state machine in src/game/stratagems.ts; Scene feeds arrow presses while N is held, sim.ts owns beacon flight/blast and friendly fire. Why: input rules stay testable and deterministic, and strike effects stay server-replicable events, not UI state.
- Class backpacks (src/game/backpacks.ts) are pure per-class modifiers on stratagem behavior (cooldown, friendly fire, supply heal, recon reach); Scene sets `sim.backpack` from the class and Operator.tsx draws the matching pack. Why: the three Operators use the same call-in codes differently without new input or progression state.
- Terrain ground color is elevation + biome + slope mask (slopeMask in src/game/terrain.ts): flats keep biome color, mid slopes show dirt, steep faces bare rock. Why: cliffs read as cliffs everywhere with no hand painting, and the rule stays pure and testable.
- Supply-road corridors are graded in heightAt (terrain.ts): the centre-line height is smoothed along each lane and the verge blends toward it; undergrowth clusters come from src/game/foliage.ts. Why: every system (mesh, convoys, player, props) still samples the one heightAt, and roads/forests read as designed instead of random.
- Regional atmosphere (src/game/atmosphere.ts) grades fog tint/thickness and sun/skylight per region and weather; Scene eases toward it, and diving adds an eased underwater murk plus the HUD water-overlay. Why: each region has its own air without touching the day clock or weather sync.
- Foliage wind runs in the vertex shader (src/game/wind-sway.ts onBeforeCompile; strength from wind.ts): canopies, undergrowth, grass and reeds share windUniforms, which Scene updates from the live weather wind each frame. Why: no per-instance JS cost, and storms visibly thrash vegetation without syncing anything.
- The reticle (src/game/crosshair.ts) is a pure per-frame state: spread ring from locomotion + bloom, a lagging barrel index, and hit/kill markers; Scene steps it, HUD only draws it. Why: weapon weight and feedback tunable and testable apart from rendering.
- Class movement kit lives in src/game/movement.ts (air jumps per class, Warlock glide, slide on Ctrl/J, sprint/slide FOV); Scene applies it. Why: Destiny-style mobility is class identity, and the rules stay pure and testable.
- Stride (src/game/movement-feel.ts) is the single walk/run cadence: Scene integrates it from real ground speed and feeds camera bob/sway/strafe roll, viewmodel sway and the Operator limb cycle through `motion`. Decorative GLBs (RegionModels) mount 4s after the terrain so they never compete with first-frame assets.
- Operators are NYX (Assassin/HUNTER), GOLIATH (Destroyer/TITAN) and CIPHER (Tech/WARLOCK); internal ClassId stays TITAN/HUNTER/WARLOCK. Their ability lists live in ability-network.ts + combat-engine.ts, rules in src/game/operator-abilities.ts, and old ability ids migrate through LEGACY_ABILITY_IDS in operators.ts (applied in normalizeProgression, cloud merge and the live build). Why: old saves keep unlocks and builds, and ability rules stay testable apart from Scene.
- Body type (female/male/robot) is saved on `progression.character` with the rest of the confirmed identity; missing means male. Save & Deploy runs deployCharacter (validate -> save -> launch) behind a single-flight guard; the mission launches only after persistCharacter succeeds, and the save is idempotent per deploymentId. Why: no duplicate deploys, and a failed save never starts Mission 1.
- Far districts (Nexus City, Neon City, Thalassia) mount through NearOnly (distance-gated with hysteresis, checked twice a second). Why: their point lights and meshes past the fog line cost every lit pixel and draw call for nothing.
- Regional environmental hazards are pure, clock-sampled rules in src/game/region-hazards.ts; Scene applies gravity/speed/hull effects. Why: deterministic across clients and testable.
- PerfProbe (F3 or ?perf=1) is a DOM-only frame readout (fps, worst frame, draw calls, triangles, lights, geometries, textures); it never re-renders React. Why: performance claims need measured numbers from a real browser.
- Regional armor sets (src/game/armor-sets.ts) are pure: one set per region, 5 slots, levels 1..10, 2pc/4pc bonuses scaled by average worn level; drops roll in sim.defeatMachine and grant via claimDrops (duplicates level the owned piece); sim.armorResist/armorRegen come from the worn set. Why: loot, progression and combat agree and rules stay testable.
- Stance movement lives in movement.ts (resolveStance/stepStance/cameraDrop): crouch is held (Ctrl/V/J), prone toggles on X, sprint only when standing, slide chains into crouch. Mantle/climb is not implemented. Why: stance is class-agnostic mobility and stays pure and testable.
- Ability evolution branches (src/game/branch-effects.ts) are the single place Power (+18% effect), Control (+25% duration) and Utility (-15% energy cost) become combat rules; activateLiveAbility returns already-scaled effects and Scene reads those. Why: equipped branches visibly change combat without per-ability special cases in Scene, and the numbers stay testable.
- Enemy squads read the build through sim.mods (squadArchetype, rangedHoldFire, branchPosture), set by Scene after stepSim; stepSim must carry those fields across its per-step adaptationMods() rebuild. Why: otherwise loadout-driven enemy behavior is silently reset every step.
- Armor attributes (src/game/armor-attributes.ts) are derived from each worn piece's slot/power/set (no stored stats); Scene applies loadoutEffects() = set bonuses + attributes + mixed-gear flexibility, resistance capped at 50%. Why: no set is required, no save migration, and rules stay testable.
- Per-piece perks (src/game/armor-perks.ts) apply per worn set piece, independent of set count, and fold into loadoutEffects(); set looks and body fit live in src/game/armor-look.ts and ArmorMotif.tsx, passed to Operator as `look`. Why: gear stats and visuals come from the same worn pieces, and every body type gets its own fit without separate assets.
- Verdant Forest layout (trail, spawn/ambush clearings, cover, crash site, scan rules) is pure data in src/game/verdant.ts; Terrain scatter, RegionModels, colorAt and VerdantForest.tsx all read it, and solid pieces register through obstacles.ts. GLBs (PolyFoliage) are HEAD-verified, height-normalised and report to src/game/forest-assets.ts; the trees (fir/broadleaf) are authored by scripts/build-trees.py, light enough that every tier including LOW draws them; far trees are crossed leaf-textured cards; other species stay procedural on LOW. Why: the first-mission route is never blocked, a missing file is reported not silent, and weak GPUs keep their frame budget.
- Forest understory is clustered (clusterAround patches) and gated by vegetationOk() in verdant.ts; PolyFoliage `variants` uses a model's side-by-side variants only when their boxes don't overlap. Leaf litter, moss, scorch, soil and hull wear are procedural canvas textures (forest-textures.ts) because no project asset covers them. Why: plants never grow through the trail, wreck or skid, and gaps in assets are explicit rather than faked as realistic models.
- Camera look without a mouse is pure in src/game/camera-look.ts: arrow keys and the gamepad right stick (deadzone + squared curve) turn the camera on foot; arrows still steer vehicles, enter stratagem codes while N is held, and the right stick still picks weapons while the wheel is held. Why: WASD-only players and pad players can look around, and the rates stay testable.
- Forest grass is instanced crossed cards (VerdantForest GrassCards, generated clump texture) gated by patchNoise() so meadows and bare duff alternate; a short fringe feathers the trail edge and grass near the ambush clearing is capped low for sightlines. `__forestAssets()` in the browser console reports per-model load status, variants, instances and triangles. Why: no carpet, readable fights, and a build can be verified from real numbers.
- Startup is isolated from the world scene: GameCanvas phase "boot" (BootSequence, once per session via sessionStorage, silent, any input skips, shorter and fade-free under reduced motion) then "title" (MainMenu: Continue / New Game / Character / Settings / Credits). Timeline, save check and focus/pad rules are pure in src/game/startup.ts and menu-nav.ts; useMenuInput feeds keyboard + standard-mapping gamepad intents (Esc/Backspace/B-Circle = back) and never steals typing. New Game confirms only when a save exists, and nothing is overwritten until Save & Deploy in character creation. The title background is a pure-CSS cinematic (horizon art push-in, light/shadow sweeps, mist; frozen under reduced motion) — no operator or WebGL on the menu; the operator lives in Character. Why: startup can change without touching forest, combat or missions.
- PolyFoliage instances are distance-culled (src/game/foliage-cull.ts: CULL_RADIUS per species, re-selected every 0.25 s once the camera moved 6 m); full matrices are computed once and only near ones sit in the live buffer. Why: instanced meshes draw every instance (and shadow-pass them) regardless of distance, which cost millions of triangles on integrated GPUs.
- Character creation restores the saved operator through forgeInitial (src/game/deployment/forgeState.ts: per-field fallback, subclass must belong to class), asks before discarding unsaved edits (forgeDirty), shows worn armor per slot plus Defense/Mobility/Intellect from armorSummary (which only calls loadoutAttributes), and is kept mounted under Settings so a settings visit never loses edits. deploy() skips the tutorial when progression.tutorialComplete. Why: reopening Character must not silently reset or discard the player's choices, and there is one stat path.
- Quest events come from gameplay through one bridge: questEventsFromHud (src/game/quest-signals.ts) turns HUD snapshots into ENTER_WORLD/HEAT/LOCKDOWN/HACK/KILL/SURVIVED events that GameCanvas applies with a functional setProgression. gameTick runs reconcileQuests (event ledger over completedMissions/dungeonClears) so a scripted mission finished before its quest is active is never lost; the starter vehicle is derived from saved selectedVehicle (mission-gates.ts). Why: the fd- chain used to stall at fd-02 and returning players re-picked a vehicle; quest-progression.test.ts replays fd-01→fd-18 with only emitted events.
- Scripted-mission resume lives in src/game/missions/persistence.ts: `progression.activeMissions` holds each machine's minimal run (phase, target, hack/hold, wave2Done, NOVA line), saved by GameCanvas effects through `withMissionRun` and restored by `restoreMission` only when `canResume` (not completed, tutorial done, prerequisite done, System Core only on fd-18). Rewards go through the idempotent `applyMissionCompletion`; live drones/bosses are never saved, Scene respawns the wave/boss from the phase. Why: reload resumes a mission without double rewards and without a second persistence system.
- Verdant relief and crash set-dressing: extra forest hills and the impact pit are one pure function (src/game/forest-relief.ts) added inside terrain.ts rawHeightAt, faded to nothing on the spawn, trail, ambush clearing and crash pad, so mesh, player, AI and props share the ground (terrain mesh spacing is 2.5 m, so keep feature wavelengths >= 8 m). Hull/scout/crate/rut/puddle layout and hull collision circles are pure data in src/game/crash-layout.ts; CrashHull.tsx builds the (procedural, no GLB) geometry. Why: no shader-only displacement, decorations can never block the mission route, and every placement is testable.
- The authored operator GLBs have no materials or textures, so OperatorModel paints them: src/game/operator-paint.ts maps Mixamo bones to armor regions (helmet/chest/pauldron/gauntlet/thigh/shin/boot/suit/gear) and builds the palette from the worn set pieces (armor-look.ts) or the operator's armor colour; OperatorModel writes it as vertex colours blended by skin weights on a private geometry copy. Why: a single flat material read as grey/black and showed no armor; worn gear now changes the model, and plating is lifted so it is never near-black.
- Onboarding/session restore: the live operator (cls/subclass/appearance/bodyType) is always derived from the save via sessionFromProgression (src/game/session-restore.ts) at mount, in enterWorld (deploy + Continue/hub share it) and in adoptProgression (cloud pull/merge, New Game); live mission machines survive a merge, idle ones adopt the merged run (reconcileRuns). The intro plays once per save (progression.introSeen, set on first deploy), the tutorial checkpoint is progression.tutorialRun (saved every step, cleared at VICTORY, merged as furthest step). New Game with a save parks it in a free slot via beginNewGame (save-slots.ts) and is refused when all slots are used; it never erases. Worn armor is chosen per slot in the forge Gear tab (forgeState slotOptions/cyclePiece) and saved with the character. Why: one source of truth for who the player is, no onboarding replay, no silent data loss.
- Unique Scenario bosses (src/game/unique-scenarios.ts) can carry an authored GLB (`model`) and a walk-in `lair`; waypoints.ts SCENARIO_LAIRS feeds the map and Scene summons them with summonScenarioBoss. ScenarioBosses.tsx downloads a model only once its boss is summoned, and WarMachines keeps the procedural body until `scenarioModelReady` has the id, so a failed or slow GLB never hides the boss. Boss GLBs live in public/models/bosses (textures shrunk to 2K JPEG colour + 1K normal/roughness). Rime Alpha (frost wolf, Frostspire) and The Dark Knight (Wastelands) are the first two; glacierFang/nullPlate are placeholder drop materials until the real boss items are defined. Why: more one-off encounters without new trigger plumbing or a hard dependency on a heavy asset.
- Unique Scenario gimmicks are pure rules in src/game/scenario-gimmicks.ts (poise, adaptive element-learning, attuned element cycle, close-range duel); sim.ts applies the multiplier on top of the poise window for bosses with a scenarioId, announces Hollow Saint attunement changes, and clears scenarioId/gimmick state in defeatMachine and summonBoss because machine slots are pooled. Why: gimmicks stay testable apart from rendering and a reused slot never inherits a previous boss's rules or model.
- Scenario signature loot lives in src/game/scenario-loot.ts: SCENARIO_LOOT per scenario (guaranteed GearItems + chance entries with named chances, e.g. DARK_KNIGHT_MANTLE_CHANCE), granted by grantScenarioReward through claimDrops. sim.defeatMachine attaches a ScenarioClaim (run id minted at summon, `participated` = PARTICIPATION_HITS bullets landed, chance rolls pre-drawn); claims are idempotent through progression.earnedRewards ("scenario-run:<id>", cloud-merged by union). Duplicates level the owned copy, then convert to fracture shards. GearItem gained optional `rarity` and `perk`; the Null Sovereign perk (null-disruption.ts) is a pure charge→pulse rule applied in the sim bullet loop (stun + boss poise; there is no enemy-shield system). Local single-player only: not server-authoritative, no fireteam layer. Why: one reward path, replay-safe, and no fake claims about combat that is not wired.
- Class abilities (Q/E/R, or the controller chord View+X/Y/B) are cast only through `castAbility` in src/game/ability-effects.ts: it validates cooldown/energy/target, spends via live-build.ts, applies effects to the real sim (damage goes through `applyMachineDamageMods` in sim.ts so bullets, abilities and turrets share Weaken/Marked, boss poise, scenario gimmicks and reward participation; stuns go through `stunMachine`, which caps bosses), and appends `AbilityEvent`s (CAST/REJECTED/CANCELLED) to `sim.abilityEvents`. Scene only forwards input and pose; HUD reads `abilityHud(live)`; VFX/animation must read events, never apply effects. Death/respawn calls `cancelAbilities`. Why: abilities are testable without rendering and presentation can never spend energy or change enemies. Mapping and known gaps: docs/ability-integration.md.
- Unique Scenario encounters (Dark Knight, Rime Alpha, Drowned Monarch, Hollow Saint) are authored data in src/game/scenario-encounters.ts and run by src/game/encounter-sim.ts from stepSim: every attack is TELL -> geometry test at impact via hurtPlayer -> RECOVER with a weak-point window; hazard zones damage/slow by position (`sim.hazardSpeedMult`, applied in Scene); the last phase's finale opens an explicit window and an hp floor (checked in defeatMachine) stops earlier kills; death calls resetEncounters; Hollow Saint decoys are inert `decoy` machines that award no kill/credit/loot and expose the real boss when all are shattered. EncounterFx only reads `sim.encounterZones/encounterEvents`. Unbroken Glass, System Core and Red Ronin intentionally keep poise/gimmick-only behaviour. Why: attacks stay deterministic/testable, VFX never decides hits, and rewards still flow through the one idempotent scenario-run claim. Local single-player only; no fireteam/server layer exists.
- Story scenarios share one layer, src/game/story.ts (`progression.story`: flags, decisions, trust, stages, collectibles; effects are once-keyed so replays never farm trust; cloud merge = union/max/furthest stage, newer copy wins decisions). Branching dialogue is data (`DialogueGraph`) shown by StoryDialogue.tsx; scenario rules live in pure modules (vaelith.ts: stages, memories, lair defence, trust ladder Hostile/Wary/Allied/Bonded, companion strikes) and Scene only forwards world events (`onStoryEvent`) that GameCanvas applies through those reducers. Non-lethal encounters declare a `truce` in scenario-encounters.ts (hp floor, no attacks, boss withdraws, never a kill or claim). Story rewards use the existing claim ledger with stable ids (`story:vaelith:alliance`/`bond`), never a boss kill. Why: choices must change real state, replays must be safe, and no second dialogue/quest/reward framework exists. Docs: docs/story-scenarios.md; aerial warfare is audit-only (docs/aerial-warfare-audit.md).
- Regional walk-up shops are pure rules in src/game/regional-shops.ts (placement, specialist stock, level/story gates, material pricing); Scene reports the nearby shop, ShopWindow trades. Why: shops stay testable and priced in persisted materials.
- Sun/moon/star positions and brightness are pure functions of the day clock in src/game/celestial.ts (8-day lunar cycle); SkyBodies.tsx draws a sun-lit moon, halo and fading starfield, and Scene's moonlight follows the same moon angle. Why: phases and light direction always agree with what is visible.
- Rivers, lakes and waterfalls are traced downhill on the uncarved heightmap in src/game/rivers.ts (springs on upper slopes, spill over saddles, pool in basins, falls only on real drops) and carved into heightAt by terrain.ts; Rivers.tsx only draws them. Why: water can never float, flow uphill, or disagree with what players walk on.
- Regional signature sky effects (ash, aurora, spores, dust, pollen) are pure in src/game/sky-effects.ts; Scene eases a live copy and SkyFx.tsx draws it in shaders. Why: borders blend and every client agrees without syncing.
- Terrain shaping goes into naturalHeightAt (terrain.ts) only; the water network is traced on it afterwards and rivers.test.ts guards the named features (Frostspire 4-fall ocean stream, Ember falls, Wastelands dry wash, Solara oasis). Road grading never fills a river channel; crossings become bridge decks via deckAt/walkHeight. Why: prettier land must never silently break the rivers.
- World loot caches + side contracts live in src/game/loot-caches.ts; opens are "cache:<id>@<day>" entries in earnedRewards, contracts pay through rewardMission, and every mission payout adds a missionBox. Why: reuses the union cloud merge, no new persistence, and fresher clears roll better boxes.
- Materials, glow caps, real-world scale and regional looks come from src/game/visual-standard.ts; the audited asset list lives in src/game/asset-inventory.ts and drives the dev-only /dev/assets inspector. Why: one consistent art standard instead of per-category guesses.
- Visible armor comes only from src/game/armor-pieces.ts (equippedPieces): each worn slot yields pieces parented to Mixamo bones in OperatorModel; stats stay in armor-attributes. Why: one source for appearance, pieces are swappable for authored GLBs.
- Loot box gear comes from rollCacheGear in loot-caches.ts and is appended to progression.inventory by openCache. Why: one reward handler, no second inventory.
- Enemy appearance comes from src/game/enemy-visuals.ts (faction × role → model, height, motion style); FactionEnemy falls back to the procedural EnemyModel. Why: combat/AI never depend on art.
- Wildlife rules are pure: behaviour in src/game/wildlife.ts (graze/wander/alert/flee, herd alarms, birds take off and land, vultures soar, fish school; gait phase advances with distance travelled) and placement in src/game/wildlife-habitat.ts (`buildPopulation`: per-region herds scaled by WORLD_SCALE, never in water or on the first-mission trail; fish only in lakes; `isWet` keeps land animals out of water). Wildlife.tsx only poses jointed rigs from the live critter and hides animals beyond view range; the fox is a real GLB (public/models/animals/fox.glb, credited) with a procedural fallback. Why: animals used to slide with frozen legs, fish swam through dirt and birds walked; now every rule is tested.
- Clip-less skinned quadrupeds (the Rime Alpha frost wolf) walk through src/game/skeleton-gait.ts: legs are found from the rest skeleton (feet = low leaf bones, hips = below the girdle branch, pairs share a girdle) and swung about the body's side axis in each bone's parent space; ScenarioBosses drives it from real ground speed. Why: the boss glided with locked legs.
- Authored WAVs (src/game/audio-files.ts) play through playSample in audio.ts for rifle/shotgun/tank shots, melee, big blasts, rift abilities, level-up, engine start and regional ambience loops; the procedural sound plays until a file decodes or if it fails. Why: real recordings where we have them, no silent gaps.
- Jet-boots (skate-boots.ts) are owned via earnedRewards "boots:<id>", bought with fracture shards at the Neon skate shop; the worn pair and touch input are client-local (client-input.ts). Kill Playlist songs live in IndexedDB on the device (kill-playlist.ts) and audio has separate music/ambience/weapon/ability buses. Why: device choices never enter cloud progression.
- Account stash (src/game/stash.ts): collectibles/stims are progression.materials (stims capped at 3, active buffs client-local in client-input.ts); vault is progression.vaultIds (newer copy wins); sold/dismantled gear leaves a "gone:<id>" tombstone in earnedRewards that mergeStash applies so cloud merges never resurrect it.
- Combat feedback classes (light/heavy/ability/boss) and the per-frame particle budget live in src/game/combat-feedback.ts. Why: effects scale with the attack and crowds never exceed the budget.
- Armor shaders are appearance-only: ownership is `shader:<id>` in earnedRewards, application is GearItem.shader (src/game/armor-shaders.ts); exotics keep their own glow. Why: cosmetics can never change stats, and owned shaders are reused for free.
- Vyrak's Covenant (src/game/dragon-covenant.ts) bonds the Wyrmling to the winning class via `covenant:vyrak:<CLASS>`; Vyrak is a Solara lair scenario (unique-scenarios.ts + scenario-encounters.ts, finale-gated), its story runs through boss-stories.ts, claimDrops bonds on a participated kill, covenant abilities are TACTICAL nodes at cost 999 (bond-only), and Scene renders the Wyrmling when bonded. Why: per-Operator rewards survive cloud merges, and the bond only ever comes from a real confirmed kill.
- Neon City districts (Aegis Spire, The Grid, Rust-Muck) and the Rust-Muck race are pure layout/rules in src/game/neon-districts.ts; raised walkways register as decks in src/game/district-decks.ts, which walkHeight folds in. Why: player, enemies and vehicles share one ground and race rules stay testable.
- Vyrak and the Wyrmling share the code-authored DragonModel.tsx (no external GLB to fail); encounter telegraph audio/rumble and combat-feedback camera kick are driven from Scene reading sim events. Why: presentation never writes combat state.
- Vehicle handling is one pure per-type stepper in src/game/vehicle-handling.ts (jet/heli/boat/sub/tank/car) with surface grip from vehicle-surface.ts; Scene only applies it. Why: each vehicle class feels different and the rules stay testable.
- Home/decor ownership are one-time Nex ledger spends (home:/decor:) in src/game/housing.ts; placement is progression.housing, newer wins. Why: purchases never double-charge across cloud merges.
- The hub is a Destiny-style director (Director.tsx): the world map (StarMap, embedded) is home, with Killbox, Raid, Arsenal and Saves as tabs (Q/E cycle) and Resume/System on the bar. The raid tab stays locked until online fireteams exist. Why: one navigation surface; the raid is not faked as a solo activity.
- The Killbox (PvP; player-facing name — code, activity ids, the `crucible` objective kind and `crucible-win:` save keys keep the old name so saves stay valid) is pure in src/game/crucible.ts.
